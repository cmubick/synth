808Synth

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

808Synth is a synth and drum machine you play on your own device. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: it does not collect any.

What the app collects

Nothing. 808Synth does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. The app:

  • has no accounts and never asks who you are;
  • makes no network connections of its own;
  • does not use the microphone, camera, location or contacts;
  • contains no links to websites;
  • contains no advertising;
  • contains no analytics or tracking tools;
  • includes no third-party software that collects data.

Every sound is made on your device. Nothing you play leaves it.

What is stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves a small amount of information on your device only:

  • Settings: whether haptics are on and whether the screen stays on while a beat plays.
  • A note of what you have bought, so the full version opens right away before Apple confirms your purchases.
  • How many tips you've given, which only decides how full the tip jar picture looks.

This information never leaves your device through the app, and the developer cannot see it. If you back up your device with Apple's own backup services, it may be included in that backup under Apple's terms.

Purchases

The app offers a one-time unlock of the full version and optional tips. These are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. The app never sees your name, payment details or address; it only learns from Apple whether a purchase went through.

Deleting your information

Deleting the app from your device deletes everything it has stored. Purchases stay with your Apple Account and can be restored from the app's More menu.

Children

808Synth does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what is described here (for example, recording your own sounds with the microphone, or saving your songs), this page will be updated and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@tuesdaygames.com.
